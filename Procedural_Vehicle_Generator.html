Specification for Argon AI
Three.js 3D Coordinate Contract:
• Facing: +Z forward, +Y up, +X right.
• Root Node: root (handles yaw heading rotation).
• Tilt Node: tilt (child of root; handles pitch/roll lean).
• Wheel Assemblies: 4 assemblies (FL, FR, RL, RR). Each assembly has:
  → suspension (travel)
  → steer_pivot (steer yaw rotation)
  → wheel_spin (forward roll rotation: rotation.x).
• Roof Turret:
  → turret_node (yaw aim rotation: rotation.y)
  → turret_pitch (pitch elevation/recoil)
  → muzzle_L, muzzle_R (bullet spawn anchors)
  → muzzle_flash_L, muzzle_flash_R (flash meshes).
• Exhaust Anchors: exhaust_anchor_L/R (nitro flame anchors).
• Secondary Mounts: rocket_pod_L/R.
• Materials: paintMat, accentMat, gunmetal, chrome, rubber.
